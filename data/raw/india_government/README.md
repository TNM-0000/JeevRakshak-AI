# Indian Government Livestock Disease Raw Data Documentation

## Source Description
- **Publishing Authority**: Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying, Government of India.
- **Data Portal**: Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India ([data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in)) & DAHD Official Portal ([dahd.gov.in](https://dahd.gov.in)).
- **Catalog Resource Title**: "Incidence of Livestock Diseases In India" (NID: 88575).
- **Annual Report**: DAHD Annual Report 2025-2026 (Published April 2026).

## Files Acquired
1. `incidence_of_livestock_diseases_in_india.csv`
   - Official historical incidence time-series (2005 to 2011).
   - Records annual outbreaks, attacks (cases), and deaths across priority livestock diseases in India.
2. `DAHD_Annual_Report_2025_26.pdf` (12 MB, 309 pages)
   - Official policy and surveillance report.
   - Includes Chapter 6: *Livestock Health* (pages 111–136), documenting the National Animal Disease Control Programme (NADCP), LH&DCP, ASCAD, state diagnostic network laboratories (NRDDL, SRDDL, WRDDL, ERDDL, NERDDL), and recent FMD/LSD progress.

## Purpose in JeevRakshak AI
- Provides long-term macro baseline for disease incidence and case fatality rates across India.
- Validates trends: FMD outbreaks declined from >2,000 annually to localized sporadic clusters following NADCP biannual mass vaccination campaigns.
- Informs the historical context component of JeevRakshak's evidence pipeline.

## Limitations
- **Not for Real-time Surveillance**: The historical incidence table covers 2005–2011; it represents longitudinal epidemiological baseline rather than current district outbreak tracking. Current tracking relies on ICAR-NIVEDI forewarning bulletins and district field reports.
- **Aggregated National Scope**: Disease records in the baseline CSV are at the national level rather than granular village level.
