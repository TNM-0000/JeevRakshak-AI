## Epidemiological Coverage
- **Geography**: All 28 States and 8 Union Territories of India (district-level resolution).
- **Diseases Forewarned (15 Major Livestock Diseases)**:
  - *Viral*: African Swine Fever (ASF), Bluetongue (BT), Classical Swine Fever (CSF), Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD), Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD), Peste des Petits Ruminants (PPR), Sheep & Goat Pox.
  - *Parasitic*: Babesiosis, Fasciolosis, Theileriosis, Trypanosomosis.
  - *Bacterial*: Anthrax, Black Quarter (BQ), Enterotoxaemia (ET), Haemorrhagic Septicaemia (HS).

## Methodological Variables Used by ICAR-NIVEDI
- Historical disease incidence data (past 20+ years from state veterinary departments).
- Meteorological variables (temperature, relative humidity, precipitation, wet days).
- Remote sensing indices (NDVI, soil moisture, land surface temperature).
- Livestock population census data (DAHD 20th Livestock Census).
